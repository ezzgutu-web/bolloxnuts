Sensory Profile

Tasting Notes

👁
Appearance

Pale straw yellow with delicate golden hues. A persistent, fine perlage rises elegantly to the surface, forming a bright, lively mousse.

🌸
Nose

Intensely aromatic with white peach, green apple, and acacia blossom. Subtle hints of pear, citrus zest, and fresh almond carry through on a clean, mineral finish.

✦
Palate

Fresh and crisp with lively effervescence. The sweetness is restrained and balanced by bright acidity. Long, clean finish with lingering notes of white fruit and cream.

A wine that captures the light of the Treviso hills — bright, alive, and quietly unforgettable.

— Estate Notes, Harvest 2023

The Land

Born in the Hills of Treviso

The Glera vine has grown in the foothills of the Dolomites for centuries. The unique combination of Alpine breezes, chalky soils, and the sheltering warmth of the Venetian plain creates conditions found nowhere else on earth.

At Bellavista, the harvest is conducted by hand in late September when the grapes reach their optimum balance of sugar and acidity. Each cluster is individually inspected before pressing.

The result is a wine that carries the character of its landscape — fresh, mineral, and quietly radiant.
